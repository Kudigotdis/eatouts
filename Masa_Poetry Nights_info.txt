Masa Poetry Nights is a highly popular open-mic spoken word, poetry, and live music event series hosted in Gaborone, Botswana. Managed by Think Tanks Advertising, the event serves as a prominent platform bringing together diverse local artists, musicians, and storyteller voices under the tagline "One stage. Countless voices."
Event Details & Structure
The sessions run as periodic evening events—often themed around cultural milestones—bringing high energy, storytelling, and deep creative expression to the city's nightlife.
• The Venue: Hosted in the vibrant Masa Square precinct in central Gaborone.
• Pricing Structure:
• Standard Ticket / Early Bird: P100
• At the Door: P150
• Students: P50
• Double Tickets: P150
• Ticketing Platform: Official digital ticketing is usually processed locally online via Ticketlinc Botswana.
The most recent landmark edition occurred on September 25, 2026, celebrating Botswana's heritage and independence spirit with the theme "Celebrating Botswana Through Words."
