FAQ
How much does it cost?
P300 one-time onboarding, then a monthly subscription that starts at P100 (Starter), P300 (Growth) or P500 (Pro). No commission on any order. See pricing.html for the full matrix.
How long until I'm live?
Typically 24–72 hours from the moment we have your menu and one good photo of each dish. Most venues are live within a week.
Who updates my page?
At launch, we do — that's the concierge model. Send a WhatsApp message with the change and we apply it. You can take over the dashboard later.
Do my customers need an app?
No. EatOuts runs in the browser — diners just open the link. Ordering happens on WhatsApp, which everyone already has.
How do orders reach me?
Straight to your own WhatsApp number. Each venue has its own routing — the customer's message arrives on your phone, not ours.
What do I need to get started?
Your restaurant name, town, a rough menu with prices, one clear photo of each dish or of the room, and your WhatsApp number. That's it.
