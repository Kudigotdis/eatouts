2. Subscription Agreement

2.1 Setup fee

A one-time P300 (Botswana Pula, three hundred) onboarding fee is payable before your listing is published to the live directory. The setup fee is non-refundable, including where you later choose to downgrade, cancel, or never go live. The fee covers concierge setup of your profile, menu, photos, contacts and first promotion.

2.2 Monthly subscription

After the setup fee is confirmed, you choose one of three recurring monthly tiers:

  • Starter — P100 / month. Directory listing, full menu, 1 active promo, 1 gallery group.
  • Growth — P300 / month. Everything in Starter, plus events & performers, 3 active promos, 3 gallery groups, reservation settings and services.
  • Pro — P500 / month. Everything in Growth, plus unlimited promos and gallery groups, and featured placement on the Promos feed.

The current monthly amount for your tier is stated on your invoice and on pricing.html. Prices are in Botswana Pula and are subject to change with 30 days' notice to the WhatsApp number on file.

2.3 Billing cycle

Your billing cycle begins on the day your listing goes live. Fees are due on the same calendar day of each month. We will send a WhatsApp reminder at least three days before the due date. Payment is by bank transfer or mobile money (MoMo) to the account on your invoice.

2.4 Publishing rule

Your listing is published to the live directory only after the P300 setup fee is confirmed as received. Until that time your listing remains in draft and is visible only to EatOuts staff and to you (via a private preview link, if one has been provided). The same gate applies to re-publishing a suspended listing.

2.5 Cancellation and downgrade

You may cancel your subscription at any time by written notice on WhatsApp to the EatOuts team. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you retain access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Downgrades between tiers take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No pro-rated refunds are given for partial months.

2.6 Suspension for non-payment

If a monthly subscription fee remains unpaid for 14 days past its due date, we may suspend your listing from the live directory. During suspension your content remains stored, but diners will not see your page. A suspended listing is re-published only after the outstanding balance and the current month are paid in full.

2.7 Ownership of content

You retain all rights to the content you supply. You grant EatOuts a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to display, store, resize, and reproduce that content for the purpose of operating the directory.

3. Merchant-Data Privacy Addendum

This addendum explains what EatOuts stores about your venue and how it is used. It sits alongside the customer-facing Privacy Policy in the EatOuts app and is consistent with it.

3.1 What we store

  • Venue profile: business name, slug, description, category and cuisine types, town / area, address, landmark.
  • Contact details: telephone numbers, WhatsApp number, email, website and social handles you supply.
  • Owner account: your WhatsApp login number and a one-way hash of your password. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • Content: your menu, promotions, events, performers, gallery images and their URLs.
  • Commercial: your tier, setup-fee-paid status, invoice history and billing dates.
  • Technical: a timestamp of the last time your listing was edited.

3.2 Why we store it

To publish your listing on the directory; to route orders and enquiries to the correct WhatsApp number; to give you access to the operator dashboard; to issue invoices and track payment; and to keep the service secure.

3.3 Who can see it

Only EatOuts team members with a business need, and only through the operator tools. We do not sell merchant data, and we do not share it with advertisers. The public directory shows only the content you have chosen to publish.

3.4 Retention

Content is retained for as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, we keep your content for 90 days in case you return, then delete it on request. Invoice records are retained for statutory bookkeeping purposes for five years.

3.5 Deletion

You may request deletion of your venue record and all associated content at any time by contacting the EatOuts team on WhatsApp. We will confirm deletion within 14 days, subject only to records we must retain by law.

3.6 Security caveat

The EatOuts operator tools run in the browser and store data in your own browser's local storage. They are a front door, not a hardened server platform. Do not treat the operator login as protection for anything more sensitive than the venue content itself.
